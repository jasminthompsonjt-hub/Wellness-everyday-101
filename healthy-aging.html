HEALTHY AGING NOTE

7 Daily Habits That Can Support Healthy Aging and Independence

Healthy aging is supported by consistent everyday choices that help protect mobility, strength, independence, emotional wellbeing, and quality of life.

Healthy Aging Video

Watch this short wellness video for practical guidance on mobility, independence, healthy routines, and quality of life.

1. Keep moving every day

Regular movement can support mobility, balance, circulation, strength, and confidence. Walking, stretching, light resistance exercise, and balance activities can all be helpful when they are appropriate for the individual.

2. Prioritize nutritious meals

Balanced meals that include adequate protein, fruits and vegetables, whole grains, healthy fats, and sufficient fluids can support energy, muscle maintenance, and overall wellbeing.

3. Protect muscle and mobility

Muscle strength plays an important role in everyday independence. Appropriate strength-building activity can help support getting up from a chair, climbing stairs, carrying groceries, and moving safely throughout the day.

4. Support brain and emotional wellness

Reading, learning, hobbies, music, conversation, puzzles, creative activities, and meaningful routines can help keep the mind engaged and support emotional wellbeing.

5. Stay socially connected

Regular connection with family, friends, neighbors, community groups, or faith and social organizations can provide companionship, purpose, encouragement, and a sense of belonging.

6. Make sleep a priority

Consistent sleep routines and adequate rest can support energy, concentration, mood, recovery, and daily functioning.

7. Keep up with preventive healthcare

Routine medical visits, recommended screenings, medication reviews, dental care, vision care, hearing checks, and preventive health discussions can help identify changes early and support informed decisions.

Remember: Healthy aging is not about perfection. Small, consistent habits can help support strength, independence, confidence, and quality of life over time.
