Our mission

Practical wellness for everyday life.

Wellness Everyday 101® is a wellness-focused platform dedicated to practical education and resources that support healthier everyday living.

About Jasmin Thompson

Jasmin Thompson is an author and the founder of Wellness Everyday 101®. Through The Canine Longevity Blueprint, she provides dog owners with accessible, evidence-informed information designed to help them make thoughtful decisions about nutrition, exercise, preventive care, and lifelong wellness for their canine companions.

Why Wellness Everyday 101

The Wellness Everyday 101 approach is built around a simple principle: wellness is supported through consistent, informed choices made over time. The goal is to make trustworthy information approachable, useful, and easy to put into everyday practice.

Growing beyond one book

The Canine Longevity Blueprint is part of a broader Wellness Everyday 101 vision: practical wellness education for different stages of life, with future resources designed to support families, caregivers, older adults, and companion animals.
