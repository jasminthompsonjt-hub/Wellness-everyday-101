PET WELLNESS NOTE

5 Daily Habits That Can Support Your Dog’s Long-Term Health

Small choices made every day can contribute to your dog’s overall health and quality of life. While every dog has individual needs, these five habits provide a practical foundation for lifelong wellness.

Pet Wellness Video

Watch this short wellness video for practical guidance on supporting your pet's health and wellbeing.

1. Provide balanced nutrition

Feed a complete and balanced diet appropriate for your dog’s age, size, activity level, and health needs. Keep treats and table foods from replacing nutritionally balanced meals.

2. Encourage daily movement

Regular physical activity helps support healthy body weight, mobility, cardiovascular fitness, and mental stimulation. The amount and intensity should be appropriate for your dog.

3. Maintain a healthy body condition

Monitor changes in your dog’s weight and body condition. Excess weight can place additional stress on joints and may contribute to other health problems.

4. Make preventive veterinary care routine

Regular veterinary examinations, dental care, vaccinations when appropriate, parasite prevention, and recommended health screenings can help identify problems earlier.

5. Support mental and emotional wellbeing

Dogs benefit from companionship, enrichment, play, training, exploration, adequate rest, and predictable routines. Mental wellness is an important part of overall canine health.

Remember: Healthy aging is built over time. Consistent nutrition, appropriate exercise, preventive care, enrichment, and attention to changes in your dog can help support wellbeing throughout every stage of life.
