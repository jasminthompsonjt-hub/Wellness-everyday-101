The book brings the major elements of lifelong canine wellness together in one practical guide. It is designed to complement—not replace—the individualized care provided by your veterinarian.

  • Nutrition and superfoods for dogs
  • Foods to avoid and life-stage feeding
  • Healthy weight and exercise
  • Preventive veterinary and dental care
  • Gut health and the microbiome
  • Joint health and mobility
  • Brain health and emotional wellness

Designed to be used—not just read

Readers can use the trackers and worksheets to record progress, monitor wellness routines, prepare questions for veterinary visits, and build a personalized plan as their dog’s needs change.

Veterinary note: This publication is educational and is not a substitute for veterinary diagnosis, treatment, or individualized medical advice. Always work with a qualified veterinarian regarding your dog’s health.
